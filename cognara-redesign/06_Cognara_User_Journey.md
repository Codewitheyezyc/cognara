# Cognara Blueprint — Document 06
# User Journey
### The Complete Experience from First Visit to First Achievement — Redesigned

> *This document describes exactly what a user experiences at every stage of their Cognara journey. It is the bridge between strategy and product. Every screen, every interaction, every message described here must be designed to move the user one step closer to their goal — and one step closer to recommending Cognara to someone they know.*
>
> *This document is the primary specification for Antigravity. Build from this.*

---

## The Journey at a Glance

```
STAGE 1 — Discovery        Someone hears about Cognara or finds it
     ↓
STAGE 2 — Landing          They land on the homepage
     ↓
STAGE 3 — Onboarding       They sign up and define their goal
     ↓
STAGE 4 — First Session    They experience Cognara working for them
     ↓
STAGE 5 — Daily Habit      They build a consistent learning routine
     ↓
STAGE 6 — The Wall         They almost quit — Cognara carries them through
     ↓
STAGE 7 — Breakthrough     They build real momentum and identity
     ↓
STAGE 8 — Achievement      They complete something meaningful
     ↓
STAGE 9 — Expansion        They start their next goal and tell someone
```

Every stage has a specific emotional state, a specific user need, and a specific product response. None of these stages can be skipped or left to chance.

---

## Stage 1 — Discovery
### How Users First Hear About Cognara

**The user's situation:**
They have a goal. They have been struggling to find a clear path to it. Something — a social media post, a friend's recommendation, a Google search, a tweet — points them toward Cognara.

**What must be true at this stage:**
The message they encounter must immediately speak to their situation. Not to Cognara's features. To their feeling.

The feeling is always a version of:
> *"I want to achieve something but I don't know how to get there and I'm tired of wasted attempts."*

**The message that works:**
> *"Whatever you want to achieve, Cognara builds your path and keeps you on it."*

Or for a social post:
> *"Stop learning randomly. Start achieving deliberately. Cognara builds your personalised roadmap and walks it with you every day."*

**The goal of Stage 1:**
Get them to the homepage. One click. One tap. That is all.

## Stage 2 — Landing
### The Homepage Experience

**The user's emotional state:**
Cautious optimism. They have clicked. Now they are evaluating in the first 10 seconds whether this is worth their time.

**The question in their head:**
> *"Is this actually different from everything else I've tried?"*

**What the homepage must do:**

The homepage has one job: convert a skeptical visitor into a signed-up user. Everything else is noise.

---

### Homepage Structure

**Section 1 — The Hero (Above the fold)**

The first thing a visitor sees must answer: *what is this, and why should I care?*

```
HEADLINE:
"Your goal. Your roadmap. Your AI mentor."

SUBHEADLINE:
"Tell Cognara what you want to achieve. It builds your 
personalised learning path, teaches you at your exact level, 
and keeps you accountable every single day."

PRIMARY CTA BUTTON:
"Start Your Journey — It's Free"

SECONDARY ELEMENT:
A short animated preview showing: user types a goal → 
roadmap appears → first lesson opens.
(No autoplay audio. Clean, fast, 10 seconds.)
```

**Section 2 — The Problem (Make them feel seen)**

```
HEADLINE:
"Sound familiar?"

THREE CARDS:
1. "You started learning but lost the thread after week two."
2. "You have 47 browser tabs open and still don't know where to begin."  
3. "You asked ChatGPT to teach you. It helped once. Then forgot you existed."

CLOSING LINE:
"Cognara is the system that fixes all three."
```

**Section 3 — How It Works (Simple, visual, fast)**

```
HEADLINE:
"Three steps to your goal"

STEP 1 — Tell Cognara what you want to achieve
STEP 2 — Get your personalised roadmap in under 60 seconds
STEP 3 — Learn every day with an AI mentor that remembers you

CTA: "See it work — Start free"
```

**Section 4 — What Users Are Achieving (Social Proof)**

```

**Section 5 — The Comparison (Answer the ChatGPT question)**

It remembers your goal. It tracks your progress. It knows 
when you're struggling. It adapts when you fall behind.

```

**Section 6 — Pricing (Simple, clear, honest)**

```

Quizzes and streak tracking
Start free →

PRO PLAN  
₦4,500 / month
Unlimited goals
All depth levels
Projects and certificates
Offline access
Priority Spark (AI mentor)
Start Pro →

**Section 7 — Final CTA**

```

"You have a goal. Let's build the path."

**What the homepage must never do:**
- List technical features before stating the outcome
- Use AI jargon ("adaptive algorithms," "NLP-powered")
- Show more than one primary CTA at any point
- Load slowly — every second of load time costs conversions
- Feel corporate or generic

## Stage 3 — Onboarding
### From Sign-Up to First Lesson in Under 2 Minutes

**The user's emotional state:**
They signed up. They are hopeful but impatient. They want to see the product working — not fill out forms.

**The cardinal rule of Cognara's onboarding:**
> *Get the user to their first lesson within 2 minutes of signing up. Every second beyond that is a conversion risk.*

---

### Onboarding Flow (Step by Step)

**Step 1 — The Goal Question**
*Screen: Clean, focused, minimal*

```
"What do you want to achieve?"

[Large text input field]
Placeholder: "e.g. Become a frontend developer, 
               Master social media marketing, 
               Pass my ICAN exams..."

Suggested goals (tappable chips below):
[Become a Developer] [Social Media Marketing] 
[Business Strategy] [Data Analysis] [Other]

Button: "Build My Roadmap →"
```

*No name. No email verification wall. No demographic form. Goal first.*

**Step 2 — Quick Context (30 seconds)**
*Screen: Three focused questions, one at a time*

```
Question 1:
"How familiar are you with [their goal]?"
○ Complete beginner — I'm starting from zero
○ Some knowledge — I've explored this before  
○ Intermediate — I know the basics, want to go deeper

Question 2:
"What describes you best?"
○ Student
○ Working professional
○ Entrepreneur / Business owner
○ Career changer
○ Self-taught learner

Question 3:
"How much time can you dedicate per day?"
○ 15–20 minutes
○ 30–45 minutes
○ 1 hour or more
```

**Step 3 — Roadmap Generation**
*Screen: Visual, exciting, fast*

```
[Cognara logo animates]
"Building your personalised roadmap..."

[Progress bar — completes in under 60 seconds]

Text beneath (rotating, while loading):
"Structuring your learning phases..."
"Ordering topics from foundation to mastery..."
"Calibrating to your level..."
"Almost ready..."
```

**Step 4 — Roadmap Reveal**
*Screen: The most important screen in the product*

```
"Your roadmap is ready, [First Name]."

[Visual roadmap display — phases and modules visible]
Phase 1: Foundation
  Module 1.1 — [Topic]
  Module 1.2 — [Topic]
  Module 1.3 — [Topic]

Phase 2: Core Skills
  [Locked — complete Phase 1 to unlock]

Phase 3: Advanced Application
  [Locked]

Estimated completion: [X weeks] at [their chosen pace]

[Large button]: "Start Phase 1 →"
```

*This screen must feel like a gift. The user asked for a path. Here it is — their path, with their name on it, structured and ready. This is the moment Cognara earns their trust.*

**Step 5 — First Lesson**
*Screen: Clean reading experience*

```
[Phase 1 — Module 1 — Lesson 1]
[Title of the first topic]

[Lesson content — at their stated depth level]

[Reading time estimate: "5 min read"]

[Progress bar at top showing position in lesson]

[Spark button — always visible in corner]
"Ask Spark anything about this lesson"
```

**Step 6 — First Quiz**
*Immediately after the lesson*

```
"Let's check your understanding"

[3–5 questions on the lesson content]
[Hearts system visible — 3 hearts to start]
[One question at a time — not all at once]

On completion:
[Score displayed]
[XP earned animation]
[Streak started: Day 1 🔥]
[Button]: "Continue to next lesson →"
```

**What must never happen in onboarding:**
- Asking for a credit card before delivering value
- Showing a tutorial overlay before the user has experienced anything
- Asking more than 3 context questions
- Making the user wait more than 90 seconds for their roadmap
- Letting the session end without starting the first lesson

## Stage 4 — First Session
### The Experience That Determines Whether They Come Back

**The user's emotional state after completing onboarding:**
Energised. They have a roadmap. They completed a lesson. They earned XP. Their streak is at 1. They feel like they started something real.

**What must happen before they close the app:**

```
END OF FIRST SESSION SCREEN:

"Day 1 complete. 🔥"

What you did today:
✓ Built your [Goal] roadmap
✓ Completed Lesson 1: [Topic Name]
✓ Earned [X] CXP
✓ Started your streak

Tomorrow's mission:
"Complete Lesson 2: [Next Topic Name]"
Estimated time: [X] minutes

[Button]: "Set a reminder for tomorrow"
[Button]: "Share my Day 1"
```

**The notification permission request:**
Ask for notification permission here — not on sign-up. The user has now experienced value. They have a reason to want a reminder. The conversion rate on notification permission is significantly higher at this moment than at any earlier point.

## Stage 5 — Daily Habit
### Days 2–7: Building the Routine

**The user's emotional state:**
Motivated. The habit is new and fragile. The streak feels precious.

**The daily experience must follow a consistent rhythm:**

```
DAILY SESSION STRUCTURE:

1. DAILY MISSION (shown on dashboard)
   "Today's mission: Complete Lesson [X] — [Topic]"
   Estimated time: [X] minutes
   CXP reward: [X]

2. LESSON
   [Content at their level]
   [Spark available throughout]

3. QUIZ
   [3–5 questions]
   [Hearts system]
   [Immediate feedback]

4. SESSION CLOSE
   [Progress update]
   [XP earned]
   [Streak update]
   [Tomorrow's preview]
```

**The Dashboard (what they see every time they open Cognara):**

```
TOP SECTION:
[User name] | Level [X] | [CXP] points
Streak: [N] days 🔥

MIDDLE SECTION — TODAY'S MISSION:
[Goal name]
[Phase X — Module X]
"Continue: [Lesson Title]"
[Progress bar: X% of current phase]
[Big button]: "Continue Learning"

BOTTOM SECTION — QUICK STATS:
Lessons completed: [N]
Quizzes passed: [N]  
Current phase: [Phase name]
Estimated completion: [Date]
```

**What the dashboard must never show:**
- A cluttered feed of options
- Multiple competing CTAs
- Anything that distracts from the one primary action: continue learning

## Stage 6 — The Wall
### Days 8–21: The Most Critical Stage

**The user's emotional state:**
Doubt. Friction. The novelty has worn off. Life is busy. They missed a day. Then another. The streak broke. They feel guilty. Guilt becomes avoidance.

**This is where Cognara must work hardest.**

---

### When a Streak Breaks

**Wrong response (what most apps do):**
```
"Your streak is gone. Start again."
[Generic motivational quote]
```

**Right response (what Cognara does):**
```
SPARK MESSAGE:

"[Name], I noticed you missed yesterday. 

That's okay — life happens.

Here's what you've already built:
✓ [N] lessons completed
✓ [N] quizzes passed
✓ [X]% of your [Goal] roadmap done

That progress doesn't disappear.

Ready to pick up where you left off?
I've put together a short 10-minute session 
to ease you back in.

[Button]: "Jump back in — 10 minutes"
[Button]: "Remind me later"
```

**The re-entry session:**
When a user returns after a gap, their first session back must be shorter and easier than normal. Not because we are lowering standards — because we are lowering the barrier to re-entry. A 10-minute win is better than a 45-minute session they never start.

---

### Proactive Re-Engagement

If a user has not logged in for 2 days:

```
NOTIFICATION:
"[Name], your [Goal] roadmap is waiting. 
Pick up where you left off — 15 minutes today 
keeps your momentum alive. 🔥"
```

If a user has not logged in for 5 days:

```
SPARK MESSAGE (in-app on return):
"[Name], it's been a few days. 

I've looked at where you are in your roadmap
and adjusted tomorrow's mission to help you 
ease back in.

You've already done the hard part — starting. 
Let's not let that work go to waste.

[Button]: "Show me my adjusted plan"
```

If a user has not logged in for 14 days:

```
EMAIL:
Subject: "Your [Goal] roadmap is still here, [Name]"

Body:
"You started something real.

[N] lessons completed. [X]% of your roadmap done.

That progress is still here, waiting for you.

I've built you a quick re-entry plan — 
just 3 sessions to get your momentum back.

[Button]: "Pick up where I left off"
```

---

### When a User Fails a Quiz Repeatedly

If a user fails the same quiz three times:

```
SPARK ACTIVATES:

"I noticed this quiz has been tricky. 
That tells me something — not about you, 
but about how the concept was explained.

Let me try explaining [Topic] a different way.

[New explanation using a different approach/analogy]

Take your time. Then try the quiz again when you're ready.

[Button]: "Retry the quiz"
[Button]: "Ask Spark another question about this"
```

## Stage 7 — Breakthrough
### Days 22–60: Momentum and Identity

**The user's emotional state:**
They pushed through. They have built a real habit. They are no longer just someone who signed up — they are someone who is becoming something.

**What the product must do at this stage:**

**Show them their growth:**
```
MILESTONE SCREEN (at 25% roadmap completion):

"You've completed a quarter of your [Goal] roadmap.

When you started, you knew [nothing / a little] 
about [subject].

Today you have completed [N] lessons, 
passed [N] quizzes, and spent [X] hours 
building real knowledge.

That is not nothing. That is momentum."

[Share button]: "Share my progress"
[Continue button]: "Keep going"
```

**Introduce projects:**
At the end of each phase, a project assignment appears:

```
PHASE COMPLETION → PROJECT UNLOCK:

"Phase 1 complete. Time to apply what you know.

Your project: [Project Title]
[Brief description — practical, achievable, 
 relevant to their goal]

This project will:
✓ Test everything you learned in Phase 1
✓ Give you something real to show for your work
✓ Prepare you for Phase 2

[Button]: "Start my project"
```

**Phase completion certificate:**
```
CERTIFICATE SCREEN:

"[User Name] has successfully completed
Phase 1: [Phase Name]
of the [Learning Goal] roadmap on Cognara.

[Date]
[Cognara seal]

[Download certificate]
[Share on LinkedIn]
[Share on Twitter/X]
"
```

## Stage 8 — Achievement
### The Moment That Builds the Company

**The user's emotional state:**
Pride. Completion. A genuine sense of having become something they were not when they started.

**This moment must be designed with the same care as the most important screen in the product.**

```
ACHIEVEMENT SCREEN:

"[User Name]. You did it.

[Goal] — Complete.

You started on [date].
You finished on [date].
[N] lessons. [N] quizzes. [N] projects. [N] days.

You are no longer someone who wanted to 
[achieve goal].

You are someone who did.

[Full roadmap completion certificate — shareable]
[Button]: "Share my achievement"
[Button]: "Start my next goal"
[Button]: "Tell us your story" ← testimonial request
```

**The testimonial request:**
```
"Your journey could inspire someone who is 
exactly where you were on Day 1.

Would you share what Cognara helped you achieve?
(Takes 2 minutes. Means everything to the next learner.)

[Text input]: "What did you achieve with Cognara?"
[Submit] [Maybe later]
```

These testimonials become the social proof that drives the next wave of users.

## Stage 9 — Expansion
### Starting Again and Telling Someone

After completing their first goal, the user is presented with two natural next steps:

```
"What's next for you?

[Start a new goal on Cognara]
Popular next goals for [Goal] completers:
• [Related goal 1]
• [Related goal 2]  
• [Related goal 3]
• Choose my own goal

— or —

[Share Cognara with someone]
"Know someone with a goal and no clear path? 
Send them to Cognara."
[Copy referral link]
[Share on WhatsApp]
[Share on Twitter/X]
```

**The referral moment:**
This is the most natural moment to ask for a referral. The user has just achieved something real. They are proud. They are grateful. They know exactly what Cognara did for them. The ask feels natural — not transactional.

## The Journey Summary: What Every Stage Needs

| Stage | User Emotion | What Cognara Must Do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — Discovery | Searching, hopeful | Speak to their situation, not our features |
| 2 — Landing | Skeptical, evaluating | Earn trust in 10 seconds, one clear CTA |
| 3 — Onboarding | Impatient, curious | Goal → roadmap → first lesson in under 2 min |
| 4 — First session | Energised, cautious | End with a win and a clear next step |
| 5 — Daily habit | Motivated, fragile | Consistent rhythm, visible progress, daily mission |
| 6 — The Wall | Doubtful, guilty | Compassion, re-entry without shame, short win |
| 7 — Breakthrough | Confident, growing | Show growth, introduce projects, raise the bar |
| 8 — Achievement | Proud, complete | Make it a moment — celebrate, certify, ask for story |
| 9 — Expansion | Satisfied, open | Next goal or referral — both feel natural |

## Non-Negotiable Experience Standards

These are the performance and experience standards every stage of the journey must meet.

| Standard | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Roadmap generation time | Under 90 seconds |
| Homepage load time | Under 2 seconds on mobile |
| First lesson access | Within 2 minutes of sign-up |
| Lesson length | 5–15 minutes reading time (based on depth) |
| Quiz length | 3–7 questions per lesson |
| Re-engagement response time | Within 48 hours of last login drop-off |
| Certificate generation | Instant on phase/goal completion |
| Mobile experience | Fully functional on all screens — Nigeria is mobile-first |
| Offline access (Pro) | Core lesson content available without internet |

## Engineering Implementation Notes

*For Antigravity — build in this order:*

**Priority 1 — Onboarding Flow Redesign**
- Goal input screen (Step 1)
- Context questions (Step 2, one at a time)
- Roadmap generation loading screen (Step 3)
- Roadmap reveal screen (Step 4)
- First lesson auto-launch (Step 5)
- First quiz (Step 6)
- End of session summary screen

**Priority 2 — Dashboard Redesign**
- Daily mission prominent at top
- Single primary CTA: "Continue Learning"
- Progress bar tied to roadmap, not just lessons
- Streak display
- Remove all clutter — one screen, one action

**Priority 3 — Re-engagement System**
- Streak break response (Spark message, not penalty)
- 2-day absence notification
- 5-day absence Spark message
- 14-day absence email
- Shortened re-entry session logic

**Priority 4 — Achievement Moments**
- Phase completion screen with certificate
- Full goal completion screen
- Shareable certificate generation
- Testimonial request flow
- Referral link generation
