# How to Use Cognara 🎓

Welcome to **Cognara**, the adaptive cognitive learning platform designed to help you build structured paths, stay consistent, and master any goal you set. 

This guide outlines all the features available on Cognara, how they function, and how you can get the best out of the app as a **Free** or **Pro** user.

---

## 🌟 The Core Philosophy: Active Learning & CXP
Learning is not just about passive consumption; it is about active recall and consistency. Cognara measures your progress through:
- **CXP (Cognara Experience Points)**: Earned by completing daily lessons and passing quiz assessments. CXP represents your verified learning achievements.
- **Streak**: Tracks the consecutive days you have showed up to learn. Building a streak keeps you accountable and builds long-term learning habits.

---

## 🛠️ Features Breakdown & How They Work

### 1. Goal Setup & Roadmap Generation
*   **How it works**: When you type in a learning goal (e.g., *"Learn TypeScript basics"* or *"Understand financial modeling"*), Cognara's cognitive engine designs a personalized roadmap.
*   **Phases & Modules**: Roadmaps are divided into **Phases** (broader milestones) and **Modules** (specific lessons) to help you digest concepts systematically without feeling overwhelmed.
*   **Free Users**: Get access to standardized learning roadmaps for their selected goals.
*   **Pro Users**: Unlock **custom generated roadmaps** that adapt exactly to your background knowledge, timeline, and preferred study pace.

### 2. Interactive Lessons & Assessments
*   **How it works**: Each module contains a focused, rich-text lesson. Once you finish reading, you are tested with an interactive **Quiz** to verify your comprehension.
*   **XP Rewards**: 
  - Completing a lesson awards **CXP**.
  - Passing a quiz on the first try awards bonus **CXP**.
*   **Free & Pro**: Available to all users.

### 3. Streak Insurance (CXP Spending)
*   **How it works**: If you break your learning streak due to a busy day, you can spend **150 CXP** to restore it!
*   **The Rules**:
  - Available to **both** Free and Pro users.
  - Maximum of **once per month** per user.
  - You must have the 150 CXP available in your account balance.
  - The streak must have been active before breaking (you cannot restore a streak that was never started).
  - Must be redeemed within **24 hours** of the streak breaking, after which the offer expires.
  - Insurance uses do not stack.

### 4. Progress & Analytics Dashboard (Pro)
*   **How it works**: Go to the **Progress** tab to view your complete telemetry data:
  - **Activity Heatmap**: A 24-week grid mapping your daily study actions (every green dot is a day you showed up).
  - **Score Trajectory**: A line chart graphing your last 10 quiz percentage trends.
  - **Topic Strengths & Weaknesses**: A bar chart identifying concepts you score high on and highlighting areas needing review.
*   **Free Users**: Standard summary stats (completed lessons, streak records).
*   **Pro Users**: Full access to all charts, heatmaps, and breakdown analytics.

### 5. AI Coach Insights (Pro)
*   **How it works**: The AI Coach analyzes your study telemetry, quiz performance, and active roadmaps to provide actionable recommendations.
*   **Pro Users**: Receive regular personalized coach insights advising you on what topics to review, how to optimize your study habits, and celebrating milestones.

### 6. Referral Rewards
*   **How it works**: Share your unique referral link (available in the **Invite Friends** tab or avatar dropdown menu) with friends.
*   **The Reward**: When your friend signs up and completes their first lesson, **both of you receive +200 CXP**!
*   **Free & Pro**: Available to all users.

### 7. Certificates of Completion
*   **How it works**: Upon completing a Roadmap Phase, you earn an official Certificate of Completion.
*   **Sharing**: You can download your certificate as a high-quality PDF or share your achievement directly to LinkedIn and Twitter/X.
*   **Free & Pro**: Available to all users.

## 💡 How to Get the Best Out of Cognara

### 💡 For Free Users:
1.  **Protect Your Streak**: Since you can only use Streak Insurance once a month, try to establish a fixed study time. Enable the **Daily mission reminders** in your Profile settings.
2.  **Earn CXP Early**: Take quizzes carefully! High scores maximize your CXP, ensuring you always have at least 150 CXP saved up in case of an accidental streak break.
3.  **Invite Friends**: Getting friends to join is a fast way to get an extra **+200 CXP** boost to fund your streak insurance or flex your status.
4.  **Download Certificates**: Celebrate your wins. Share your Phase certificates on LinkedIn to build your professional portfolio.

### 🚀 For Pro Users:
1.  **Check AI Coach Insights Regularly**: Let the coach review your patterns. If it suggests a lesson needs review, click the quick link to revisit it before moving on.
2.  **Leverage custom roadmaps**: Input specific goals tailored to your job search or class exams rather than general topics.
3.  **Study Your Weakness Chart**: Focus on topics listed in your "Needs Attention" bar chart. Keeping your average quiz accuracy above 80% guarantees optimal retention.
